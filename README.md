### About me 👋

PhD student in Electronic and Computing Engineering (PG/EEC) at the **Instituto Tecnológico de
Aeronáutica (ITA)**, with a Master's degree in Electrical and Computer Engineering and a
Technologist degree in Industrial Mechatronics. I previously worked as a substitute professor at
the Universidade Federal do Ceará – Sobral campus, and have several years of experience in
research and technological development projects.

My current research is in **computer vision and autonomous systems**: object detection and
multi-object tracking, drone-based perception, multimodal (RGB + thermal) sensor fusion, and
dataset curation/benchmarking for applied machine-learning pipelines.

### Research focus 🔭

- Object detection & tracking — YOLO, SAHI (sliced inference), ByteTrack, deep Re-ID
- Autonomous systems & UAV/drone perception
- Multimodal sensor fusion (RGB + thermal classification)
- Dataset curation, benchmarking and evaluation pipelines
- Applied machine learning & computer vision for real-world platforms
